About The Platform

Empowering Communities Through Smart Civic AI

CiviNexa AI is an intelligent civic problem reporting, classification, and tracking ecosystem engineered to make municipal management responsive, transparent, and data-driven.

Our Vision

To eliminate bureaucratic delays in public grievance redressal by replacing slow paper files and manual triage with real-time AI classification, pinpoint geographic mapping, and open status tracking.

Our Mission

To provide every citizen with an effortless, transparent reporting tool that routes civic issues (potholes, garbage, sewage leaks, unlit streets) directly to the right municipal engineers in milliseconds.

AI Architecture

Intelligent Natural Language Grievance Triage

Civic complaints submitted by citizens often contain localized expressions, urgent hazard descriptions, and varied vocabulary. CiviNexa AI incorporates a weighted keyword and semantic classifier module:

  • Text Normalization: Strips noise, parses words, and handles multi-term phrase matches.
  • Category Scoring: Matches against domain taxonomies (Roads, Waste, Sewage, Power, Water, Infrastructure).
  • Urgency Detection: Detects dangerous indicators like live electric wires, open manholes, or major pipe bursts to escalate priority to Urgent.
  • Smart Fallback: If confidence is below threshold, complaints are safely flagged as "Needs Manual Review".
