How CiviNexa AI Solves Civic Problems

From submission to on-ground municipal action in three streamlined steps.

1. Snap & Describe

Citizens enter the complaint details, pinpoint the exact location on our Leaflet map, and upload photographic evidence directly from phone or PC.

2. AI Classification

The CiviNexa NLP engine analyzes the issue text, computes confidence scores, and automatically assigns it to the exact responsible civic department.

3. Action & Live Tracking

Receive a unique Complaint Tracking ID. Track administrative inspection remarks, field worker dispatch, and resolution timelines with 100% transparency.
